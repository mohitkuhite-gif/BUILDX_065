The problem

  • Scholarship information is scattered across dozens of portals and notice boards, so eligible students miss deadlines.
  • Teachers track attendance on paper, so students sliding toward dropout go unnoticed until it's too late.
  • Rural and municipal-school students rarely hear about ITI courses, internships or entry-level jobs nearby.
  • Study material online is often heavy, ad-filled and unusable on a slow connection.

What EduConnect does

  • Matches students to scholarships instantly, using marks, income, category and location.
  • Gives teachers a live attendance dashboard with automatic low-attendance warnings.
  • Flags rising dropout risk early with a transparent, explainable scoring model.
  • Lists ITI courses, internships and local jobs students can search and filter.
  • Hosts lightweight notes that load fast, even on 2G.

Six tools, one login-free platform
